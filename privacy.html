In short

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking services and loads no content from third parties. Fonts are served from this server.

Hosting

The website is provided through GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address in server logs. I have no influence on the scope and retention period of these logs; they follow GitHub's policies. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: my legitimate interest lies in providing this website securely and efficiently. Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which is covered by an adequacy decision of the European Commission. You can find more information in GitHub's privacy statement.

The domain is managed by Porkbun LLC (USA). No visitor data is processed by Porkbun when you visit the website; Porkbun only resolves the domain via DNS.

Contact by email

If you write to me, I process your details to answer your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering requests) or, if your request aims at a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. The address support@plimsolltools.com is forwarded to my mailbox through the email forwarding service of Porkbun LLC (USA); your message and sender details technically pass through Porkbun. I delete your request once it has been fully answered, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing (Art. 21). To exercise them, contact support@plimsolltools.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, for example the one where you live or the one responsible for me: Unabhängiges Landeszentrum für Datenschutz Schleswig-Holstein (ULD), Holstenstraße 98, 24103 Kiel, Germany.
