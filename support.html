Support

Questions, bugs, ideas.

For all Plimsoll Tools products, write to support@plimsolltools.com. I usually reply within a few days.

For bug reports, please include

  • The product and its version
  • Your setup: device and OS version for apps, Unity and package versions for Unity tools
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
  • A screenshot or the error message, if there is one

Exact steps to reproduce the problem, or a small sample project for Unity tools, are the fastest way to a fix.
