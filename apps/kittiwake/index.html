Kittiwake

Split receipts fairly. No cloud, no account.

Coming soon to Google Play.

What it does

Scan receipts
Reads the total and purchase date from a photo or an e-receipt PDF. You can correct both before saving.
Split fairly
Everyone in the household has a share, 50/50 or 60/40, and single receipts can be split differently.
Monthly settlement
Shows who pays whom how much. Mark the month as settled or carry the balance over. Report as PDF or CSV.
Sync without a server
Phones sync directly via Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, or with a sync file. To join, scan a QR code once in person.

Your data stays with you

  • No account, no cloud, no server. The app has no internet access.
  • Text recognition runs on the phone.
  • Sync and backups are encrypted.
  • No ads, no tracking.
